about flashflow

import or paste a bankuse csv fields like front/back, q/a/tag, stem/answer/explanation, a/b/c choices, accepted/answers, or text with [[cloze]].
reveal, quiz, and rateclick, tap, or press space to reveal. quiz choices grade on tap. choose 1 again, 2 hard, 3 good, or 4 easy.
typing modetype an answer and press enter. manual mode waits for your rating. auto-rating proposes a rating that you can correct.
swipe behaviorswipe left to skip and unrate. swipe right to return to the previous card or reopen its rating review.
keyboard shortcutsspace reveals, enter submits typing, 1/2/3/4 choose or rate, arrow right skips/forwards, and arrow left goes previous.
review stepsturn on timed review steps to send rated cards away until they are due again. leave it off to review by filters.
minimal and fullscreenminimal mode hides manager chrome and persists after reload. fullscreen starts only from your click or tap.
export cards vs progresscards csv exports the active bank. progress json saves banks, settings, ratings, queue, skipped state, and history for restore.
cloze syntaxwrite the [[answer]] inside text. the front shows a blank line, and reveal shows the hidden answer.
