How to Play

1. Teams

The class is split into Left Team and Right Team.

2. Starting Team

The roulette appears only on Question 1. If there is a tie at the end, it appears again for the tie-breaker.

3. Alternating Questions

After the roulette result, questions alternate by team.

4. Guessing and Solving

A correct letter gives +1 point. A wrong letter removes one heart and switches the turn. Solve is only available at the start of a question. It is disabled after any letter is entered, after any wrong letter is guessed, or after one solve attempt. A correct Solve gives +1 point for each unique letter in the answer and completes the question. A wrong Solve removes one heart and switches the turn.

5. Lives

You have 6 red hearts: head, left arm, right arm, body, left foot, then right foot.

6. Bonus

When a question is solved or revealed, the Bonus button glows. The game master may award +3 points.
