:::answers
# Cell Biology Answer Key
Q1. B. Nucleus
Q2. C. Mitochondrion
Q3. True
Q4. Cell division. Accept mitosis when the lesson context specifically refers to nuclear division in eukaryotic somatic cells.
Q5. A. Nucleus
Q6. B. Ribosome
Q7. C. Mitochondrion
Q8. D. Cell membrane
Q9. Any two: separates internal contents from the external environment; regulates movement of substances; supports cellular homeostasis.
Q10. A satisfactory response should state that the membrane separates internal contents from the environment and regulates movement of substances into and out of the cell.
